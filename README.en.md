## Featured projects

### Judicial Precedent Management

An application designed to transform a linear document collection into a searchable, organized and versioned knowledge base, supporting knowledge management and precedent consultation.

**Main demonstration features:** free-text search, filters by subject and status, detailed views, history consultation, identification of inactive records, content copying and browser print-dialog export.

**Technologies and concepts:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript and JSON in the demonstration; Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, versioning and administrative permissions in the reference architecture.

**Status:** public demonstration using fictional data, no login required and read-only interface. Code is maintained in a separate repository.

[Project overview](./projetos/gestao-de-precedentes/README.md) · [Case study](./projetos/gestao-de-precedentes/estudo-de-caso.md) · [Live demonstration](https://alessandragarcia4640.github.io/gestao-de-precedentes-Demo/)

### Gabinete+

Final undergraduate project in Systems Analysis and Development, focused on creating a digital platform to organize routines, support leadership transitions and preserve institutional knowledge in appellate judges' chambers.

**Problem addressed:** knowledge loss, rework and administrative discontinuity during management transitions.

**Main deliverables:** initial requirements, user profiles, activity catalog, navigation flows, wireframes and an initial technical structure. Task management, checklists and temporary substitutions are planned features.

**Technologies and concepts:** TypeScript, React Native, Expo, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Prisma, requirements gathering, process modeling and knowledge management.

**Status:** Final Project I completed; requirements and wireframes documented, with implementation at an early stage. Public presentation uses fictional examples; development code is kept in a private repository.

### Information Security Fundamentals

Three team-based laboratory projects completed at the University of Coimbra, connecting network security, digital identity and web-application protection.

**Main deliverables:** segmented network with firewall and monitoring; remote access through VPN with certificates and multi-factor authentication; comparison of web testing before and after the introduction of a WAF.

**Technologies and concepts:** CentOS, VirtualBox, IPTables, Suricata, OpenVPN, OpenSSL, PKI, OCSP, PAM/TOTP, Kali Linux, OWASP ZAP/WSTG, Juice Shop, Apache, ModSecurity and OWASP CRS.

**Status:** academic laboratory activities completed. Public documentation presents architecture, results and limitations without claiming production experience or independent validation of the tests.

[Laboratory overview](./projetos/seguranca-informatica/README.md) · [WAF comparison](./projetos/seguranca-informatica/trabalho-3-seguranca-web.md) · [Evidence and limitations](./projetos/seguranca-informatica/evidencias-e-limites.md)

### Data Science and Artificial Intelligence

Academic prototype for automotive-workshop data management, connecting data import, a relational database and integration with a classification model to support analysis of maintenance-review needs.

**Main deliverables identified in the code:** JSON and CSV import, type conversion, modeling of five SQLite tables, license-plate queries, layered organization and integration for generating and storing predictions.

**Technologies and concepts:** Python, SQLite, SQL, CSV, JSON, Joblib, relational modeling, ETL and integration with a machine-learning model.

**Status:** academic submission with code and report, reviewed through documentation. Full execution and predictive performance have not yet been validated in this review; documented consistency adjustments remain.

[Project overview](./projetos/ciencia-de-dados-ia/README.md) · [Architecture](./projetos/ciencia-de-dados-ia/arquitetura.md) · [Evidence and limitations](./projetos/ciencia-de-dados-ia/evidencias-e-limites.md)

**Technologies and concepts:** digital government, cybersecurity, risk management, interoperability, data protection and institutional capacity.

**Status:** study and academic paper completed during the academic mobility period at the University of Coimbra.
